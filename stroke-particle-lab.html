How to read this test
Every dab is a real rounded 3D particle. Spacing controls overlap. Surface jitter moves whole particles sideways across the drawing plane. Depth jitter moves them out of that plane. At zero jitter and tight spacing, the particles should merge visually into a smooth stroke.
Important
This is a particle behavior prototype, not yet connected to the production brush. Orbit the camera to inspect whether the particles retain their 3D volume.
