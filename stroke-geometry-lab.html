Pass condition
Orbit all the way around the vertical stroke. It should remain a clean, solid black rounded brush mark with a constant diameter and round ends. No lighting bands, texture, halftone, hatching, or toon shading.
Important distinction
This test isolates a true 3D round brush volume. From the side it should read as a 2D round brush mark. Looking directly down its length will naturally reveal its circular end.
